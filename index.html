Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What hospitality programs can I explore?

We can discuss options in hotel management, culinary arts, restaurant operations and related hospitality disciplines. Specific program availability depends on the institution.

Can I apply without hospitality work experience?

Some entry-level programs may accept students without prior experience, depending on academic and language requirements. Contact us to review your background.

Do programs include internships or placements?

Practical training or placement opportunities vary by institution and program. We can help you review confirmed details before applying; internships and jobs are not guaranteed.

How much are tuition fees and living costs?

Costs depend on the institution, program, country and intake. We can help you request current official fee and estimated cost information.
